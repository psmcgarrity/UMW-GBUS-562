### System

Wildframe Coffee Roasters is a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. Brand voice: warm, plainspoken, and a little irreverent, with no corporate jargon. Customers: environmentally conscious urban professionals.

### You

Wildframe wants new perks for subscribers who stay six months or longer. Generate 6 perk ideas. 
Required format: a numbered list of exactly 6 ideas, each one sentence of no more than 25 words.

### Claude

1. After six months, you get first dibs on small-batch micro-lots before they're even listed on the site.

2. Free shipping for life, because loyalty shouldn't cost you more than your coffee habit already does.

3. A reusable Wildframe canvas tote, because you're clearly not a single-use kind of person.

4. Annual "Roast Your Way" session where you help pick a seasonal blend straight from our cupping table.

5. 10% off merch and gear, forever, no asterisks, no fine print, no corporate nonsense.

6. A handwritten thank-you note from whoever roasted your beans that week, because robots don't say thanks.
