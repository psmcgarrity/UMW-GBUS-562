## Oxono rules in brief

- The 6×6 board has two shared totems, X and O. Each player has 8 X pieces and 8 O pieces in their colour.
- On your turn, slide one totem any distance up, down, left or right (it can't pass over anything), then place one of your pieces with that totem's symbol on an empty square next to it.
- Win by lining up four pieces in a row or column that are all your colour, or all the same symbol in any colour. Diagonals don't count.
- A totem boxed in on all sides may jump over pieces to the first empty square. If no square next to the totem is empty, you may place your piece anywhere.
- If all 32 pieces are placed without a line of four, the game is a draw.
