How to play
  • Each player has 16 pieces: 8 marked X and 8 marked O. You're pink, the computer is black.
  • On your turn, move one totem (X or O) any distance up, down, left or right. It can't pass over pieces or the other totem.
  • Then place one of your pieces with that totem's symbol on an empty square right next to it (not diagonally).
  • Win by lining up four pieces in a row or column that are all your colour, or all the same symbol in any colours. Whoever completes the line wins. Diagonals don't count.
  • If a totem is boxed in on all four sides, it can jump over the pieces in a straight line to the first empty square. If it can't jump anywhere, it can move to any empty square.
  • If there's no empty square next to the totem, you may place your piece on any empty square.
  • You can only move a totem if you still have pieces of its symbol. If all 32 pieces are placed with no line of four, it's a draw.
  • To change your mind after moving a totem, click it again (or press Esc) before placing your piece.
